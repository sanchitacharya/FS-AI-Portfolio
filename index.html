About me

I’m a robotics engineer in training — studying BEng (Hons) Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Hertfordshire, and applying that work through engineering student projects and clubs.

At UHRA (University of Hertfordshire Racing Autonomous) I’m Software Lead: planning and control for our Formula Student AI car, with a push toward stronger sensing and localization for the full competition mission set.

At STRIDE, our robot football team, I’m working on odometry-based localization so the robot can keep a reliable sense of where it is on the pitch. More from that project will land here as it develops.
