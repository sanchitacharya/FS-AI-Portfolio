01 · About

About me

I’m a robotics engineering student on the BEng (Hons) Robotics and Artificial Intelligence course at the University of Hertfordshire. Alongside the degree I build real systems through student engineering teams — turning coursework into software that has to run on hardware.

As Software Lead at UHRA (University of Hertfordshire Racing Autonomous), I own planning and control for our Formula Student AI car. The next priority is stronger sensing and localization so we can take on the full competition mission set with confidence.

On STRIDE, our robot football team, I’m developing odometry-based localization so the robot can track its position on the pitch as it moves. More from that work will appear here as it matures.
