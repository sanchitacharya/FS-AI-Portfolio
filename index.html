About me

I’m a student engineer focused on robotics and autonomous systems. As Software Lead for University of Hertfordshire Racing Autonomous (UHRA), I work on the software that plans and drives our Formula Student AI car — and I’m building toward stronger sensing and localization for the full mission set.

Outside the race team I also work on robot football and related robotics projects. I care about systems that hold up outside the simulator: clear problems, honest results, and a path to make the next run better.
